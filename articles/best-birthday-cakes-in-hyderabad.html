Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How do I order a birthday cake in Hyderabad?

Order at www.krispies.in/menu, WhatsApp +91 79752 18850, or walk into any of our 5 outlets. Custom cakes need 24-48 hours' notice.

Q2: Can I get same-day birthday cake delivery in Hyderabad?

Yes. Order ready-stock cakes before 11 AM for same-day delivery. Express 1-hour delivery available within 5 km of any outlet for an extra fee.

Q3: What is the best birthday cake flavour for adults?

For adults, Chocolate Truffle, Tiramisu, Red Velvet, and Lotus Biscoff are the top choices. For older adults, Rasmalai fusion cake or pineapple cream cake are popular comfort options. Read full flavour guide.

Q4: What is the best birthday cake for kids in Hyderabad?

For kids, theme cakes like Unicorn, Spiderman, Princess Castle, and Jungle Safari are most popular. Flavour-wise, chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry are universal favourites.

Q5: Are eggless birthday cakes available in Hyderabad?

Yes. Krispie's defaults to eggless and offers every flavour in eggless format with no compromise. Read our eggless cakes guide.

Q6: What is the average price of a birthday cake in Hyderabad?

At Krispie's: ₹299 for a basic 500g vanilla cake to ₹2,499 for a premium 2kg custom theme cake. Custom designs from ₹899. Wedding-size birthday cakes can go up to ₹15,000+.

Q7: Can I get a custom photo birthday cake in Hyderabad?

Yes. Krispie's offers edible-photo printing on fondant discs placed on buttercream cakes. Send a high-resolution photo via WhatsApp; 24 hours' notice required. Price: from ₹499.

Q8: Can Krispie's write a name on the birthday cake?

Yes, included at no extra cost in any language (English, Telugu, Hindi, Urdu, etc.). Provide exact spelling in writing when ordering.
