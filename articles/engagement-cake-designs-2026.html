Flavour Choices for Engagement Cakes

Most Romantic

  • Red Velvet with cream cheese (most popular by far)
  • Strawberry shortcake
  • Chocolate hazelnut praline
  • Vanilla with rose-pistachio cream

For Indian-Style Engagements

  • Rasmalai cake
  • Saffron-pistachio sponge with rabri cream
  • Motichoor laddoo cake

Size Recommendations

Engagement cakes are typically smaller than wedding cakes because the gathering is more intimate.

  • Family-only ceremony (20-30 people): 2 kg cake
  • Engagement party (50-80 people): 3-4 kg cake or two-tier
  • Large engagement reception (150+ people): 6-8 kg cake or three-tier

Engagement Cake Toppers Trending in 2026

  • Gold or rose-gold acrylic "Mr & Mrs To Be"
  • "She Said Yes" with confetti illustration
  • "Forever and Always" calligraphy
  • Personalised name initials in 3D fondant
  • Two interlocking edible gold rings

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the best engagement cake design in 2026?

Trending engagement cake designs in 2026: single-tier fondant cakes with pressed sugar flowers, pastel ombre buttercream, gold drip with floral crown, and minimalist initials in calligraphy. Heart shapes and two-tier cakes are also popular.

Q2: How much does an engagement cake cost in Hyderabad?

Engagement cakes at Krispie's start from ₹899 for a 1 kg decorated cake. Premium fondant single-tier cakes: ₹1,499–₹2,999. Two-tier: from ₹2,999. Custom sculpted designs are priced on request.

Q3: How early should I book an engagement cake?

Book at least 72 hours in advance for standard designs. Custom fondant or two-tier engagement cakes need 5–7 days. For weekend or festive-season engagements, book 10–14 days ahead.

Q4: Can I get matching engagement cake and cupcake sets?

Yes. Krispie's offers engagement cake + cupcake sets where the cupcakes match the main cake's colour and design. Popular options include 12 or 24 cupcakes paired with a 1 kg engagement cake.

Q5: What flavours are popular for engagement cakes?

Rose and Lychee, White Chocolate Raspberry, Belgian Chocolate, Butterscotch, and Red Velvet are the top flavours for engagement cakes at Krispie's. Lighter, floral-inspired flavours are trending in 2026.

Q6: Can I add a photo to an engagement cake?

Yes. Krispie's prints edible couple photos on fondant or wafer sheets and places them on the cake. Send a clear, well-lit photo in high resolution via WhatsApp. 48-hour notice required for photo cakes.
