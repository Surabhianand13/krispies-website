1. Belgian Chocolate Overload 🏆 #1

The undisputed bestseller. A dark Belgian chocolate sponge, soaked in espresso syrup, layered with dark ganache, and topped with chocolate shards and a rich truffle drip. If you need a cake that impresses every time, this is the one. Available same-day at all outlets.

Why it's trending: It photographs brilliantly, satisfies hardcore chocolate lovers, and works for every occasion from birthdays to corporate events.

2. Creamy Rasmalai Cake

India's answer to the classic cream cake. A soft vanilla sponge soaked in saffron-infused rabdi, layered with malai cream, and topped with pistachio shards, rose petals, and actual rasmalai pieces. This is the cake for people who love mithai in Western dessert form.

Why it's trending: Cultural pride in Indian flavours is driving a major shift away from purely Western cake formats. Rasmalai Fusion hits that sweet spot.

Price: From ₹849/kg · Order Rasmalai Cake →

3. Butterscotch Dream

A Krispie's classic since 1996. Caramel-cream layers, house-made butterscotch praline, and a light vanilla sponge. Still the most nostalgic, most comforting cake in our range — and it never stops selling.

Why it's trending: Nostalgia marketing is huge in 2026. Customers want the cakes they grew up eating, made properly. This is that cake.

Price: From ₹749/kg · Order Butterscotch Dream →

4. Red Velvet Royale

A three-layer deep crimson velvet cake with tangy cream-cheese frosting, filled with a thin layer of fresh berry compote. The dramatic colour makes it the most Instagram-documented cake we make.

Why it's trending: Red Velvet has been popular for years, but 2026's version leans harder into the cream-cheese tang and the crimson vs white visual contrast.

Price: From ₹1,199/kg · Order Red Velvet Royale →

5. Galaxy Theme Cake

A customised design — typically a dark buttercream or black fondant exterior with metallic purple, blue, and gold hand-painted swirls, edible stars, and a mirror-glaze panel. Each one is unique. Huge for teen birthdays and adult milestone celebrations.

Why it's trending: Galaxy aesthetics crossed into mainstream from social media in 2024 and haven't slowed. The dark background makes every colour pop in photos.

Price: From ₹1,499/kg (custom) · Contact us to order

6. Mango Bliss Cake

A summer seasonal favourite that runs October–April. Fresh Alphonso mango pulp in the cream, mango curd between layers, and mango slices on top. Light, fruity, and impossible to resist.

Why it's trending: Mango is India's national fruit and a natural cake flavour. As customers push for more locally-rooted flavours, Mango Bliss has moved into the top 5.

Price: From ₹849/kg · Order Mango Bliss →

7. Bluey Theme Cake (Kids' #1)

The animated Australian cattle dog is the most requested children's theme at Krispie's in 2026. Typically done in blue fondant with hand-modelled Bluey and Bingo figures, or with an edible print topper. Available in vanilla or chocolate sponge.

Why it's trending: Bluey has dominated children's media globally and is Hyderabad kids' most-watched show in 2025–26. The demand for Bluey cakes has tripled since 2024.

Price: From ₹1,299/kg (custom theme) · WhatsApp to order

8. Minimalist Dried Flower Buttercream

Clean white or ivory buttercream with pressed dried flowers (pampas grass, baby's breath, dried roses) as the only decoration. No piped flowers, no fondant — just texture and botanicals. The most requested wedding and engagement cake design in 2026.

Why it's trending: Boho-natural aesthetics are dominant in weddings and baby showers. Customers want elegance without excess, and this achieves it perfectly.

9. Black Forest Bliss

The timeless classic — always trending. Chocolate sponge, whipped cream, cherry compote, and chocolate shavings. Krispie's version uses fresh cream (not non-dairy) and a tart cherry filling that balances the sweetness.

Why it's trending: Some cakes never go out of style. Black Forest is a safety-first order for multi-generational gatherings where someone will object to anything too adventurous.

Price: From ₹499/500g · Order Black Forest →

10. Lotus Biscoff Crunch (Fast Riser)

A rising star. Vanilla or brown butter sponge with Lotus Biscoff spread cream, Biscoff crumble between layers, and a caramelised Biscoff biscuit topping. New to Krispie's menu in 2026 and already in the top 10.

Why it's trending: Biscoff went viral globally post-pandemic and its warm caramel-cinnamon flavour translates perfectly to an Indian dessert palate. A genuine crowd-pleaser.

Trending Cake Designs by Occasion (2026)

Birthday (Kids under 10)

Bluey, Spiderman, Unicorn, Dinosaur T-Rex, and Cocomelon. 3D sculpted fondant figurines are more popular than edible prints in this age group — parents want the "wow" moment when the cake enters the room.

Birthday (Teens & Adults)

Galaxy theme, travel-destination cakes, career/hobby themes (cricket bat, guitar, stethoscope), and the number cake format (a large fondant "25" or "30" filled with flowers, fruits, and mini desserts). Aesthetic > character.

Weddings

Minimalist dried flower buttercream, ivory fondant with sugar roses, monochromatic white-on-white piping, and Indian fusion (rangoli pattern fondant, mehndi-inspired henna art on a cake, gold lotus motifs). Bold couture designs with gold leaf and sculpted sugar flowers for premium budgets.

Baby Showers & Gender Reveals

Pastel ombre buttercream, 'Oh Baby!' lettering cakes, cloud and rainbow themes, and gender-reveal surprise-inside cakes (pink or blue sponge revealed at cutting). Mint-green and blush palettes dominate 2026 baby shower aesthetics.

Corporate & Bulk Orders

Logo cakes (edible print), branded cupcake boxes, tier cakes for product launches, and personalised 100g mini cakes for employee distributions. Clean, professional designs in brand colours with no characters or themes.

What's NOT Trending in 2026

As much as it's useful to know what's in, knowing what's out saves you from ordering something dated:

  • Rainbow cakes with visible colour layers — peak 2018, feels dated now
  • Heavily piped rosette cakes — beautiful but overdone; customers are moving to textures
  • Artificial cream non-dairy cakes — customers increasingly ask for fresh dairy cream
  • Overly busy designs — everything on one cake (drip + flowers + fondant figures + edible photo) is out; restraint is in
  • Mirror glaze entire cakes — was trending 2019–2021, now reserved for showpieces
