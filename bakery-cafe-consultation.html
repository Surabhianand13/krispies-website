The Technology Difference

Own Your Software. Stop Renting It.

Most bakeries and cafes pay a monthly fee to Petpooja, or a similar billing/ordering tool, for as long as they're in business — and never own a line of the code. We build your ordering website, billing software and admin dashboard as a one-time project. When it's done, it's yours: the code, the database, all of it.
